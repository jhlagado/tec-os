# River viewpoints and underground levels — review proposal

The author noted that the first underground river is reached southwards and
another is later reached eastwards, making the world difficult to picture.
This is a descriptive/geographic design proposal, not an implemented map edit.

## What the current source establishes

- Round chamber 26 leads south to river ledge 27.
- Ledge 27 leads east to balcony 28, overlooking the temple cavern.
- The rope descends from 28 to the temple floor at 35.
- The gate enters crypt 37; its southern return is temple 35.
- The crypt's eastern passage reaches cell 38, then east through its shaft
  reaches cavern 39; south reaches wizard cavern 36; east reaches ledge 40.
- Room 40 is described as another high ledge above a subterranean river, with
  water entering through the cavern roof to the north.
- North from 40 reaches waterfall 45, explicitly fed by surface river Gioll.

These are access directions from different starting rooms, not statements
that the river flows south at one point and east at another. The descent also
introduces a vertical level change. The source does not establish distances,
precise river bends, or a complete surface-to-underground alignment.

## Proposed coherent interpretation

Treat the ledges as two viewpoints on the connected underground water system:
27 is an upper viewpoint near the round chamber; 40 is reached after descending
into the temple complex and travelling farther along the caves. The northern
waterfall feeds the system from Gioll. This is a candidate world layout to
verify against the full surface/drain map before final wording, not a claim
that the original already documents all of it.

Give the viewpoints distinctive names/landmarks and state their relationship.
Do not use the compass direction needed to reach a ledge as a flow bearing.
Avoid inventing an east/west or north/south river course merely from room IDs.

Suggested revised ledge-27 wording should identify the upper viewpoint and
visible north return to the round chamber/east passage to the balcony, using
the agreed repaired map. Ledge 40 should identify the later/lower viewpoint,
water entering from the northern roof opening, west return to the wizard's
cavern, north approach to the waterfall and south continuation along the ledge.
Clearly mark the abrupt eastern edge as a hazard, not an ordinary passage.
Whether that compass mistake should be blocked rather than fatal remains a
separate fairness decision; current 40E is fatal, so do not test it live.

Caverns80 retains the same vague "another ledge" description at 40 and the same
40N45/S48/W36/E-fatal exits. Its ordinary map corrections do not fix this prose.
The waterfalls, roof inlet and ledge heights should be consistent in both text
and an engineering map with vertical transitions explicitly labelled.
