CAVERNS ON TEC-OS -- COMPARISON TRIAL

Select PICO9918 at 80 columns, or use CON PICO 80.

Assembly Caverns80:
  CAVERNS
HELP lists commands. Space/Enter continues the story; Q skips.
SAVE and LOAD use CAVERNS.SAV on the current drive.
QUIT, then N, returns to the shell.

Experimental BASIC comparison:
  BAZA CAVBASIC
  CAVBASIC

The revised BAZA compiles this with the shell loaded. UNLOAD is unnecessary.
LOOK, LIST, N/S/E/W, I, GET COMPASS, DROP COMPASS, SCORE and QUIT work.
Use normal tool commands, e.g. KILL TROLL WITH SWORD or OPEN DOOR WITH KEY.
Answer N to leave or Y to restart. BASIC has no disk saves.
TED CAVBASIC.BAS reads and edits the source.

This trial shares Caverns80's revised map and completion route. The author's
preferred original compass and one-use bridge rules are under review while
the recovered drainage/return route is audited. This is not an archival
replacement or a final gameplay decision. See docs/caverns-original.md and
docs/caverns.md in the TEC-OS source tree for evidence and provenance.
