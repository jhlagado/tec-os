CAVERNS -- THE ORIGINAL BASIC ADVENTURE, REFINED FOR TEC-OS

Select PICO9918 at 80 columns, or use CON PICO 80.
Type CAVBASIC to play. Answer N after QUIT to return to the shell.

Your aim is to find the lost treasure of Svartalfheim and bring it back to
this hut. Treasure left in the hut is worth twice its carried value.
Find the treasure room, explore the temple and castle, and read their clues.
The maximum score is 126. There is no automatic ending: use SCORE or QUIT.

Commands:
  NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST (or N/S/E/W)
  LOOK, LIST (or I), SCORE, QUIT
  GET COMPASS, TAKE COMPASS, DROP COMPASS, PUT COMPASS
  USE SWORD, KILL TROLL WITH SWORD
  USE KEY, OPEN DOOR WITH KEY
  USE ROPE, DOWN WITH ROPE
  USE CANDLE, LIGHT BOMB WITH CANDLE

Take your compass: without it, directions can lead you astray. The paths
sometimes curve and double back. Opposite directions need not undo a move.
The string bridge remains usable for the homeward journey. The rope descent
is one-way. Keep your eyes open for clues that offer another way back.
A formidable locked entrance suggests the constructive use of explosives.
Off-target blasts leave your location and surroundings unchanged; the bomb
remains recoverable. The candle must be lit to ignite it.

Read or edit the BASIC source with TED CAVBASIC.BAS.
Rebuild it on this machine with BAZA CAVBASIC, then run CAVBASIC.
The compiler works with the shell loaded; UNLOAD is unnecessary.
The compiled game includes its required runtime; it does not load BAZA.RTL.
There are no disk saves. Y at the ending starts a fresh adventure.

Based on John Hardy's recovered MicroWorld BASIC for the Microbee, refined
with the author's approval. The original compass, directed map, temple,
crypt, key and treasure mechanics are retained. Documented fixes normalize
command aliases and subroutine returns. The bridge and bomb are clarified
for a complete adventure without knowledge from an earlier failed attempt.
The separate Caverns80 assembly comparison is retained for development;
it is not on this starter disk and is not the release's gameplay reference.
